REROLL

Discover hidden roguelike and roguelite gems that are quietly catching on.

Working prototype · iOS 17+ · Not yet released

REROLL is a discovery app for roguelike and roguelite games. Players swipe through games one at a time, the way a dating app works, with a fresh selection each day — surfacing titles that are gaining momentum but have not broken through yet, instead of the best-sellers everyone already owns.

This page is an overview of the app for partners and press: what it is, how it behaves, and where the project stands.

The discovery model

The core of the product is a ranking signal we built and validated ourselves, not a generic popularity chart:

  • Discovery scoreA momentum signal. Games that have only just started to get attention rank above titles with a large but static following.
  • Eligibility100+ total reviews. The lower bound filters out titles with too little signal to judge.
  • No rating filterPositive-review percentage is displayed but never used to filter. Divisive games are part of the point.
  • Catalog sizeApproximately 2,400 roguelike / roguelite titles are currently tracked. The figure moves with each nightly rebuild.
  • RefreshRebuilt nightly by an automated batch job (GitHub Actions).

These parameters are still being tuned. The thresholds, and the way cards are selected for each day's lineup, are likely to change both before and after release. The figures above describe the current build rather than a fixed specification.

Data

  • SourceGame information, images and reviews come from Steam, with attribution as required.
  • DeliveryA nightly batch job rebuilds the catalog and publishes it as a single static JSON file served over HTTPS. The app downloads and caches that file, so catalog traffic does not scale with user count.
  • PrivacyNo ads, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party SDKs. Usage measurement is anonymous. See the privacy policy for what the app talks to and what it sends.

Product and business status

  • StageFeature-complete prototype. Public release pending.
  • TeamOne independent developer, based in Japan.
  • PlatformiOS 17+, native SwiftUI.
  • Launch modelFree at launch, with no advertising and no paid tier. The first goal is simply to find out whether there is an audience.
  • MonetizationAdvertising is under consideration once there is an audience. Nothing is planned for launch.
